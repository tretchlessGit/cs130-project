# Final Project Ideas

I looked through the CORGIS datasets and found two that I thought could be interesting to work with. I'm not sure which one I'd want to use yet, but these are some questions I could look into.

## 1. [Video Games](https://corgis-edu.github.io/corgis/csv/video_games/)

I play a lot of video games, so this one caught my attention. It has information about game sales, reviews, and how long games take to finish.

- Do games with higher review scores usually have better sales?
- Do games with online multiplayer tend to sell more than games without it?

## 2. [Super Bowl Ads](https://corgis-edu.github.io/corgis/csv/super_bowl_ads/)

I also thought this dataset was interesting because it looks at different types of Super Bowl commercials and how many views they got.

- Do funny Super Bowl commercials get more views than commercials that aren't funny?
- Do ads featuring celebrities get more likes on YouTube than ads without celebrities?
